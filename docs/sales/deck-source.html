The problem

Good content is a daily job that never stops.

A single post needs an idea, a caption in the right voice, a design, the right format for each platform, a fact check and a time slot. Multiply by five platforms and three posts a day. Most small teams do it late, inconsistently, or not at all.

What the studio takes over

Ideas from your own data, captions per platform, designs and videos, the brand and fact check, scheduling. The work a person does at a desk for hours.

What stays with you

One decision per post: approve or reject. Nothing is published without it. The studio never approves its own work.

What it gets better at

Every week it measures what your posts really achieved and rewrites its playbook from the results. Its models are retrained and only replaced when the new one is proven better.

The brain

It learns from every post, and proves it before it changes.

Two loops. The fast one turns your real results into rules the writers read. The slow one retrains seven models every week and only deploys a model that beats the current one on posts it has never seen.

Loop 1: results become a playbook

1
MeasureReach, engagement and orders per post, normalised against your own 20 previous posts so growth does not fool it.
2
TestBootstrap confidence intervals per feature. Findings that the data cannot support are not shown.
3
Write the playbookPer platform: what works, how often to use it. The strategist and writers read it on the next run.
4
Check the effectThe next posts are measured again. The loop closes.

Loop 2: the judge decides who goes live

1
Pull13,871 rows from 40 tables, checksummed, plus 1.9M public posts of market knowledge.
2
Train 7 modelsWhole weekly run in 4 min 16 s.
3
JudgeThe new model faces the current one on 77 unseen posts. Seven gates, all must pass.
4
Deploy only if betterOtherwise the old model stays. This week one of two scorers was refused.

Hybrid search

Meaning (vectors) and exact words together, then a second model re-reads the best candidates. Runs locally: no search subscription.

Personal data masked

Prices, hours and claims exist in a single confirmed file. A caption that quotes anything else is blocked.

Control

Built so it cannot embarrass you.

Automation without control is a risk. Every layer below is enforced in code, not requested in a prompt.

HUMAN GATEEverything it makes is a draft. A person approves each post on a review page; nothing is published or scheduled without that click.
BRAND CHECKBlocked words, no unverified claims, no items you do not sell, prices only from the confirmed file. In the measured run 3 of 30 ideas were stopped here.
JUDGE GATESA new model is deployed only after seven gates, including no leakage of future information and no platform made worse.
BUDGET CAPA hard token limit per run (4 million) with a warning at 80%. A runaway cannot happen.
LEAST POWEREach agent sees only its own tools, runs in an empty folder, has no memory between calls and a turn limit. Secrets never reach an agent.
FAIR BY DESIGNAudiences are areas and interests, never ethnicity or religion; religious ritual words are blocked in copy.

Cost

Powerful and cheap come from the same design.

The expensive part of AI is tokens. The studio spends them only where judgment is needed, and does everything else in code or on small local models.

1
Code does the checking17 of 23 tool calls are plain code. Zero tokens.
